# Security

Igrek runs on your own computer and holds sensitive things: your AI logins, your projects' code and
`.env` files, your team's encryption keys. Security reports are taken seriously.

## Reporting a problem

Do not open a public issue. Use GitHub's private report ("Security" tab, "Report a vulnerability") or
write to mohamedattia.dev@gmail.com with:

- what is wrong and where (file, page or request);
- the smallest steps that show it, on your own install;
- what an attacker could do with it.

You get an answer within a week. Please give a fix time before telling anyone else; you are credited
when the fix ships, if you want to be.

## Rules for testing

Test only your own installation. Never test another person's Igrek, a team hub you do not run, or the
AI providers' services through Igrek.
