Igrek: know why.

A control center for AI coding agents. Igrek runs on your own computer, follows every Claude Code and Codex chat, and tells you what changed, why it changed and what it cost.

Install IgrekRead the docs

What it does

Why?

Next to every changed file: the request that led to it, the plan step it belonged to and the expert's note.

Calm chats

One box for your messages, the agent's plan and your own to-do list above it, the changed files with a one-click undo.

What a turn cost

Each turn's share of your plan's limit, in the chat's header. Everything that calls a model is marked.

Experts

Security, database, architecture and more, as read-only reviewers who know their field, and packages to share them.

Memory and lessons

Decisions and mistakes are kept per project and put in front of the AI before similar work.

Teams, encrypted

An optional self-hosted hub shares chosen projects end to end encrypted; the hub cannot read them.

Yours, on your computer

Igrek uses your own AI logins and needs no API key. It sends nothing to anyone, has no telemetry, and costs nothing. It works on Linux, macOS and Windows, with or without Docker.
